Habitox

A calm, private habit tracker for Android.

Habitox helps you build habits and stay consistent. Everything is stored on your device, and you can optionally back it up to your own Google Drive.

Features

Daily and weekly habit tracking with reminders
Goals, timeline and insights to see your progress
Journal with notes and photos
Focus timer with ambient sounds
Home screen widgets and quick settings tiles
Optional Google Drive backup and sync across your devices

Your data stays yours

Habitox has no accounts and no servers of its own. If you connect Google, your backup is saved in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only Habitox can access. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
