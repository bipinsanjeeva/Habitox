Privacy Policy

Habitox for Android · Effective date: 7 November 2026

Habitox ("the app") is a habit tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. In short: your data stays on your device, and if you choose to connect Google, a backup is stored in your own Google Drive.

Information the app handles

  • Content you create: habits, completions, goals, journal entries, notes, photos you attach, and app settings. This is stored locally on your device.
  • Google account details (only if you connect Google): your email address and basic profile, used only to show which account is connected and to authorise Drive access.
  • Google Drive backup (only if you connect Google): a backup file of your Habitox data, saved in the hidden app data folder of your own Google Drive.

How the information is used

  • To run the app and show your habits, reminders, widgets and insights.
  • To back up and sync your data between your own devices through your Google Drive.
  • To display the connected Google account in Settings.

Google user data

If you connect Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Drive app data (drive.appdata): lets Habitox read and write only its own private folder in your Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Email and profile: used only to identify the connected account.

Habitox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties. It is not read by humans except where you explicitly share it, for security, or to comply with the law.

No developer servers, ads or tracking

Habitox does not send your data to servers operated by the developer. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Permissions on your device

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to deliver habit reminders and focus timer alerts.
  • Internet: only for the optional Google Drive connection.
  • Camera / photos: only when you choose to attach a picture.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can use Habitox without ever connecting Google.
  • To remove local data, use the reset option in the app's Settings or uninstall the app.
  • To remove your Drive backup and revoke access, go to your Google Account permissions, select Habitox, and remove access. You can also manage app data from Google Drive settings → Manage apps.

Children

Habitox is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
